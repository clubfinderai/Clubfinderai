What you carry now

Look at the numbers stamped on the sole of your short clubs. If nothing is stamped, most modern pitching wedges are 44 to 46°.

A means approach, G means gap, S means sand. All three sit between your pitching wedge and your sand wedge.

A guess is fine. It only sets the spacing.
